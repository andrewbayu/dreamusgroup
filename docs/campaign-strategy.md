# Make Room for Play

Proposed strategy, 14 October 2026. This updates the proposal; it does not change DreamUs-owned websites or social profiles.

## The story and three workstreams

A working adult has a full calendar and a group chat that keeps postponing. One person makes the plan. The group tries SuperPark together. The experience becomes a reason to return.

1. **Brand campaign for the right market.** Hero film, social positioning, TikTok UGC, adult content and partnerships share one invitation.
2. **Product and pricing for repeat play.** Subscription-first pricing design for professionals; clear existing corporate programme packaging, with company-paid visit packs as a later hypothesis.
3. **Digital conversion.** Matched campaign destinations, adult offer pages, a proper corporate landing page, sales handoff and verified measurement.

TikTok is a tactic within workstream 1. Landing-page optimisation and paid media belong to workstream 3. Pricing and corporate product design belong to workstream 2.

## Hero campaign

Theme: **Make Room for Play.** Message: **SuperPark is for grown-ups, too.** Primary audience: professionals, adult friends and office teams. The brand remains all-age.

Proposed 30–45 second film: desk/meeting/postponed group chat → one colleague invites the group → arrival and safety briefing → shared challenges and honest reactions → campaign message and relevant CTA. Adult outing cut: see the after-work offer. Corporate cut: plan your team outing. No promised wellbeing, productivity or team-performance outcomes.

Deliverables: hero master, adult/corporate cuts, short vertical edits, approved stills, profile bio, pinned adult outing and corporate guides. Use approved adult footage, never pass concept illustrations off as actual venue proof.

Proposed bio:

> SuperPark is for grown-ups, too.  
> After-work play • Friends • Team outings  
> PIM & Puri • Plan your visit ↓

Adults and corporate offers already appear in the supplied ads. Consistency across bio, posts and landing pages is the recommendation. Current profile bio was not independently verified in this review.

## TikTok UGC: Clock Out. Play On.

Shared format: routine → real play → honest reaction → Make Room for Play → relevant offer. Creators keep their own voice. Select for working-adult audience fit, disclosure, participant consent and rights for paid reuse.

| Episode | Story | Destination |

No scripted endorsements presented as genuine reviews. Film genuine reactions; publish complaints or drawbacks honestly where relevant. Never equate views with bookings.

## Product and pricing

Design a repeat monthly offer first, with visit slots, access, expiry and value against tickets. Validate willingness to pay, contribution, actual use and capacity before annual or unlimited options. Prepaid access is a valid first test; auto-renewal requires payment support and consent. Existing memberships do not establish a recurring subscription.

For corporate buyers, package existing SuperTeam, SuperSquad and SuperBuilder programmes with confirmed agenda, host, group sizes, venue, inclusions, quote/invoice and terms. Company-paid repeat visit packs are a later test, not an existing product. Keep simulator assumptions visibly illustrative.

## Corporate landing page

Interactive preview is accessible from proposal chapter 14. It contains a hero, audience value, existing programme names, three booking steps, booking questions and a local enquiry prototype. Nothing is sent; there is no backend or booking integration.

Hero: **Less meeting. More playing together.** Supporting copy: “Your team deserves time beyond the desk. Make room for a hosted SuperPark outing—built around your group, venue and schedule.” CTA: **Plan your team outing.**

Production requirements: confirmed programme descriptions, group limits, session duration, price or quote process, safety/accessibility and venue rules, cancellation/payment terms, extras, approved team photos and verified organiser feedback. Keep quote help visible when dated catalogue products are unavailable.

Enquiry fields: company, work email, team size, preferred venue/date and needs. Live version needs privacy/consent copy, validation, confirmation, sales notification, a real fallback contact and an agreed response commitment. Do not publish placeholder inclusions, contact details, prices or testimonials as facts.

## Digital campaign and measurement

Adult hero/UGC → monthly subscription as the main online offer → single-ticket alternative. Corporate content/ads → corporate landing page → qualified enquiry → quote → paid booking. Segment message and destination by intent; retarget with useful product details where consent allows.

Before scaling, test mobile forms and sales handoff; validate Meta/Google events, cross-domain attribution and duplicate handling against actual orders. Track landing visits, valid/qualified enquiries, quotes, paid bookings, attendance, contribution and repeat use. Views/shares remain interest signals. No budget, targets or uplift invented without account and sales data.

Sequence: understand audience/venue economics → approve hero/offer/page → launch one venue/audience test → review bookings, costs, feedback and return visits. Leadership owns approval; Aditya coordinates; operations confirms delivery; finance confirms economics; sales owns enquiry follow-up; agreed staff/agency resources produce and run campaigns.

## Subscription-led online sales revision

Action 2 now explicitly recommends monthly subscription access as the main online adult offer, using gym/fitness membership as an analogy for a routine and a period of access. Annual access is a later step after repeat demand is established; single tickets serve occasional visitors. Corporate programmes retain their own quote route. Discount memberships are not equivalent to access subscriptions.

The visible calculator is removed. A static example uses a Rp200,000 ticket and Rp600,000 monthly subscription with four included visits. Four tickets would cost Rp800,000: Rp200,000 saved (25%), or Rp150,000 per used visit. One visit remains cheaper with a ticket.

For the same 100 customers, assume one ticket per customer without a subscription and four used visits per customer with one. Monthly sales: Rp20m versus Rp60m. At 3% fees and Rp25,000 incremental cost per visit, contribution is Rp16.9m versus Rp48.2m: +Rp40m sales and +Rp31.3m contribution. These are illustrative assumptions, not approved DreamUs prices or a demand forecast. Contribution excludes fixed costs, acquisition costs and displaced peak sales; it is not profit. Compare prices on the same tax/refund/discount basis.

Sensitivity: if customers already buy four tickets, ticket contribution is Rp676,000/customer versus Rp482,000 with the pass: Rp194,000 less. The commercial case therefore depends on extra demand, use, costs and renewal. Four included visits are capped access, not unlimited. Recurring revenue requires actual renewal; prepaid monthly access can bridge to recurring billing when systems and consent are ready.
