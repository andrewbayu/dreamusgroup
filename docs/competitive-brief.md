# DreamUs portfolio and first growth priority

Checked 14 October 2026. Decision: which DreamUs brand should lead a test aimed at working adults and corporate teams? Geography: Jakarta/Jabodetabek. Framework: target buyer → public offer → alternatives → implication.

## Recommendation

Optimise SuperPark first for this audience. Its all-age activity format already includes adults as players. Its Indonesian corporate page already sells named programmes and hosts. Start by improving the offer, sales and booking journey; test paid demand before scaling. Keep Pororo Park and Tayo Station focused on their family and learning roles.

This is a portfolio-fit recommendation, not a proven ranking of returns. The selected public offers support “children’s play has many close alternatives.” They do **not** establish that adult play has lower competitor density, that the children’s market is saturated, or that Pororo/Tayo are weaker businesses. Playtopia directly counters any claim that the adult/corporate space is uncontested.

## Method and limits

- Compared advertised target audiences, formats and booking uses. “Close alternative” is a strategic interpretation of overlap; it is not a measured win/loss result.
- Used selected official public pages. No numerical strength ratings: quality, capacity, service and conversion were not tested.
- The AEON directory returns locations beyond its requested filter. No outlet count, density ratio or market-size estimate was derived.
- Kidzilla’s location directory was readable in search-indexed official content; direct retrieval failed. Current outlet operations need confirmation.
- AEON’s FAQ includes old pandemic material. Only audience information is used. No historical prices or health rules are carried forward.
- Bounce Street’s official indexed articles mention adult/group and corporate activity, but direct retrieval timed out and article freshness was unclear. Excluded from the slide comparison rather than treated as an audited current package.
- The analysis is a selected offer scan, not a census of all indoor parks, escape rooms, sports, bowling, corporate event providers or other substitutes. Brand counts across the rows are not comparable market totals.
- Public marketing pages verify the existence of advertised offers, not their performance. No demand, profitability, employee outcomes, market share or absence of unlisted offers is assumed.

## Next decision

Review SuperPark’s actual catalogue, professional/HR enquiries, paid bookings, venue capacity and costs. Improve one existing corporate package or test one after-work session. Compare bookings, attendance, revenue after direct costs and return visits. Reconsider the priority if first-party evidence favours another brand.
